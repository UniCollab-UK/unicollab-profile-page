UniCollab

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 January 2026

Who we are and how to contact us

KYJ House Limited is the data controller responsible for your personal data collected through the UniCollab mobile application and associated services.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your rights, or have a privacy concern, please contact our privacy team at support@unicollab.co.uk. We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe we have handled your data unlawfully.

What personal data we collect

We collect information you provide when creating and maintaining your account: your name, university email address, university, course, year of study, date of birth, profile photo, skills, bio, portfolio items and any other information you choose to add.

Your date of birth is collected solely to verify that you meet our 18+ minimum age requirement. It is not displayed on your profile or shared with other users.

We collect content you create on the platform: project posts, messages, comments, applications and collaboration records.

If you enable push notifications, we collect a device push token so we can deliver them. This token is stored against your account and is used only to send you the notification categories you have enabled in Settings — it is removed if you disable push notifications entirely or close your account.

We collect usage data automatically when you use the app: the features you interact with, searches you perform, profiles and projects you view, the time and duration of your sessions, and your approximate location at the country level derived from your IP address.

We collect device and technical data: your device type, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers and crash logs.

If you choose to verify your university email or connect a third-party service, we receive confirmation data from that provider.

How we use your personal data

We use your data to create and operate your UniCollab account and to deliver the services described in our Terms of Service, including powering the Bridge matching engine, enabling messaging and collaboration, and displaying your profile to other users according to your visibility settings.

We use your data to personalise your experience, including generating project and people recommendations, skill suggestions and match scores.

Where you have enabled them, we use your device push token to deliver push notifications for messages, comments, matches, calendar reminders and other activity. You can turn push notifications on or off entirely, or by category, at any time in Settings under Notifications.

We use anonymised and aggregated data to understand how students use the platform and to improve our product and AI features. We will never use your personally identifiable data to train AI models without your explicit prior consent.

We may use your contact details to send you service communications such as security alerts, policy updates and account notifications. We do not currently send marketing or promotional emails. Settings includes a preference for this in advance of any such feature; if we introduce marketing emails in future, we will only send them to users who have explicitly opted in, and you may opt out at any time.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

Performance of a contract: processing necessary to create your account and deliver the UniCollab service as described in our Terms of Service, including verifying your date of birth to confirm you meet the 18+ eligibility requirement in our Terms.

Legitimate interests: processing necessary for our legitimate interests in keeping the platform safe, preventing fraud, improving our product through analytics, and enforcing our Terms, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Consent: processing you have specifically consented to, such as marketing emails, interest-based advertising or use of your data to improve AI features. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Legal obligation: processing required to comply with applicable law, including data retention requirements and responses to lawful requests from authorities.

AI processing and Bridge

The Bridge matching engine and other AI features analyse your profile, skills, activity and content to generate recommendations, match scores and writing assistance. This processing is core to the UniCollab service.

Text and content you submit to Bridge is processed by our AI infrastructure to generate responses. Your personally identifiable content is not shared with third-party foundation model providers for training purposes. AI processing is subject to safeguards and access controls.

AI-generated output may be inaccurate. You are responsible for reviewing it before relying on or sharing it.

Bridge produces suggestions, not decisions: it surfaces recommendations for you to review and act on yourself, and does not make any solely automated decision about you that has a legal or similarly significant effect, such as automatically accepting or rejecting you for a role. If this changes in future, we will update this policy and provide a way to request human review, as required under UK GDPR.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data to any third party under any circumstances.

We share data with service providers who help us operate the platform, including cloud hosting providers, our authentication and identity service, email delivery providers, payment processors, analytics services and AI infrastructure providers. All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and are permitted to use it only for the specific purposes we have authorised.

We may disclose your data if required to do so by law, court order or other valid legal process, or if we have a good faith belief that disclosure is necessary to prevent fraud, protect the safety of any person, or investigate a security incident.

Public profile information, including your name, university, course, skills and any portfolio items you have not marked private, is visible to all logged-in users and verified businesses on the platform.

In-person meetups and future employer access

When you connect with another student to collaborate, your profile information (course, skills and project details) is visible to that person as described above under Who we share your data with. If you arrange to meet another user in person, for example at a cafe, co-working space or campus library, any details you choose to share about that meeting, such as a meeting name, date or time, are visible only within that private conversation or project team and are never made public or shared with other users.

We do not verify the identity of other users before an in-person meeting. We recommend meeting in public places and using your own judgement, as set out in our Community Guidelines.

We are considering introducing a feature that would let verified employers search public student profiles for internship and freelance opportunities. If we introduce this feature, we will update this Privacy Policy in advance and will only share your profile with an employer if you explicitly opt in via a control in the app; your data will never be shared with employers by default or without your consent.

Cookies and tracking technologies

UniCollab uses essential local storage and session tokens to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. These are necessary for the app to function and cannot be disabled.

We use a limited set of analytics tools to understand how users interact with the platform in aggregate. This data is anonymised where possible.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers and do not currently share any activity data with advertising partners. Settings includes preferences for interest-based and partner advertising in advance of any such feature; if we introduce third-party advertising in future, it will only apply to users who have opted in, and you can change this preference at any time in Settings under Advertising data.

Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active.

If you close your account from Settings, your profile, projects, portfolio, messages and other associated personal data are permanently deleted immediately. Some data may be retained for longer periods where we have a legal obligation to do so, for example financial records required for tax purposes.

Support requests and content reports are handled differently: the content of a bug report, support message or content report (including any screenshots attached) is kept for our own record-keeping after your account closes, but it is disconnected from your identity at that point — we no longer hold a link between that record and you personally.

Anonymised or aggregated data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you may be retained indefinitely for research, analytical and product improvement purposes.

International data transfers

UniCollab is based in the United Kingdom. Some of our service providers are located in other countries, including countries outside the UK and EEA.

Where we transfer your personal data to a country that does not provide an equivalent level of data protection to the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK International Data Transfer Agreements or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the relevant authority.

You may contact us at support@unicollab.co.uk for more information about the specific safeguards in place for any particular transfer.

Your rights under UK GDPR

Under applicable UK data protection law you have the following rights:

Right of access: you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification: you may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to erasure: you may ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances, for example where we no longer need it for the purpose it was collected.

Right to restriction: you may ask us to restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances.

Right to data portability: you may request your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

Right to object: you may object to processing based on our legitimate interests at any time.

Right to withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

You can exercise most of these rights directly through Settings. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion of your account and data from a web page instead, at unicollab-uk.github.io/unicollab-profile-page/delete-account.html. For other requests, email support@unicollab.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction or alteration. These measures include encryption of data in transit using TLS, encryption of sensitive data at rest, strict access controls ensuring that only authorised personnel can access personal data, regular security assessments and monitoring for unusual activity.

We require all staff and contractors with access to personal data to comply with confidentiality obligations.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by applicable law.

Children's privacy

UniCollab is not directed at or intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We verify your age at signup by asking for your date of birth and do not permit account creation for anyone who does not meet this requirement.

If we become aware that a person under 18 has created an account, for example by providing a false date of birth, we will suspend the account and delete the associated personal data as quickly as possible.

If you believe a person under 18 has created a UniCollab account, please notify us at support@unicollab.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, our data practices or the features we offer. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

When we make material changes to how we use your personal data, we will notify you by email and via an in-app notification at least 14 days before the changes take effect.

Continued use of UniCollab after the effective date of an updated Privacy Policy constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
