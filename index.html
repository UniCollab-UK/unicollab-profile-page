About UniCollab

UniCollab is a platform for university students to team up on real projects outside the classroom — finding collaborators from other courses and universities, building a genuine portfolio of work, and growing the skills that get noticed by employers.

What you can do on UniCollab

  • Post or find a project — share something you're building and need help with, or browse what other students are looking for collaborators on.
  • Build a team — invite collaborators, track who contributed what, and keep a record of the skills each person used.
  • Grow a real portfolio — completed projects, outcomes and work samples live on your profile, not just a list of modules.
  • Sharpen your CV — AI tools score your CV against real roles, spot skill gaps, and coach you ahead of interviews.
  • Stay organised — message collaborators, schedule meetings and manage deadlines in one place.
  • Know who you're working with — accounts are verified against a real university email before they can collaborate.
