Invite rewards: the rules

How UniCollab's invite rewards work, who can take part, and how rewards are paid.

1. Who runs the offer

The UniCollab invite rewards are run by KYJ House Limited ("UniCollab", "we", "us"). By sending invites, you agree to these rules and to the UniCollab Terms of Service.

2. Who can take part

Anyone with a UniCollab account can send invites. You must be 18 or over and have verified your account with your university email address.

3. What counts as a sign-up

A sign-up counts towards your rewards when a student you invite:

  • creates a UniCollab account using your invite link or invite code; and
  • verifies their account by confirming their university email address.

Each person only counts once, and only for the person whose invite code they used. An account that is created but never verified does not count.

4. The rewards

  • £20 when 5 students you invite have signed up. These 5 can be from any university.
  • £30 more when 5 more students you invite have signed up, each from a different university, and none from a university any of your first 5 came from. Students from different universities in the same city count as different universities.

The most you can earn is £50 per person. A student's university is taken from the university email address they verified with.

5. Claiming and payment

You can see who has joined, and how close you are to each reward, under Track referrals in the app. Once you've earned a reward, tap Claim your reward.

Our support team will then email you at the address on your UniCollab account to arrange payment. Rewards are paid in pounds sterling by bank transfer to a UK bank account in your name. We may ask you to confirm your identity before paying.

6. Fair use

Invites must be sent to real students who want to use UniCollab. Rewards will not be paid, and may be withdrawn, for sign-ups that are fake, duplicated, made by you for yourself, or obtained by misleading anyone or by spam. We may close accounts involved in misuse.

7. Changes to the offer

We may change or end the invite rewards at any time. Any reward you have already earned before a change will still be paid under the rules that applied when you earned it.

8. Questions

Email support@unicollab.co.uk. You can also visit our support page.
