Frequently asked questions

How do I report a problem in the app?

Open the app, go to Settings → Help centre → Report a problem, and describe what happened. You can attach screenshots too.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account preferences → Close account. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion from this page instead.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile or a message from them, tap the menu (⋯), and choose Block or Report.

Who can sign up for UniCollab?

UniCollab is for students and graduates with a valid UK university (.ac.uk) email address, and verified businesses posting collaboration opportunities.
